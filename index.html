What is real here and what is not sources, model and validation

From the documents

  • docAll 211 rod positions, their types (АР, УСП) and the 12 height-detector cells, from Karpan's maps for 00:39 and 01:22:30 (pp. 362–363), cross-checked against INSAG-7 Fig. II-6. Plant grid labels 66…12 / 11…65.
  • docPre-accident axial flux shape (INSAG-7 Fig. II-5, curve 4); coolant flow 56 800 → 51 000 m³/h during the run-down; rundown pumps 13, 14, 23, 24 and their trips at 01:23:42.6–43.0 (Karpan App. 3).
  • docTeletype: two RTA-80s printing 7 characters per second, time without a leading zero ("1.23.39 АВАР ЗАЩИТА 5"), cut off when power failed (Karpan pp. 359–361). DREG: low-priority 2–4 s cycles numbered "№NNNД", "=0" marks a signal clearing, 9 rod positions logged once a minute (INSAG-7 p.77).
  • docPRIZMA: SKALA recalculated core parameters about every 5 minutes; the duty engineer printed PRIZMA every two hours on odd hours and carried it to the control room (Karpan p.350). ORM was never on a continuous display.
  • docThe Архив tab reproduces the surviving teletype and DREG lines of 26 April 1986.

Reconstructed or invented

  • invConsole look: the VFD tubes, the column keyboard (keys 0–7), the red and cream function keys and the Гашение switch are modelled on a photograph of a SKALA engineer's console in the computer room, whose controls (cabinet power-on, subprogram start) mark it as the duty engineer's panel rather than the SIUR's request device. Applying its style to the SIUR's device is a design choice, not a documented match.
  • invNot an emulator: the V-3M instruction set and SKALA software are not public. Request codes, PRIZMA and cartogram printout layouts, and the choice of the 9 logged rods are this simulator's own.
  • invPhysics: 28-slice axial one-group diffusion (quasi-static) with point kinetics, per-slice void, Doppler, xenon/iodine and a burnup profile; every rod's absorber, graphite displacer and water columns at its real depth, weighted by its radial position. Rods are radially averaged, so the local character of the excursion (rows 12–30, Karpan p.380) is not captured.
  • invTuned, not derived: migration area, water-column absorption, burnup profile, and a boiling-crisis threshold after which channels rupture, rods jam and steam floods the core. The xenon profile at 01:22:30 is solved from the measured flux shape because it was never measured (INSAG-7 p.81).
  • invFuel-channel positions are inferred (every lattice cell inside the core not used by a rod or detector). Their colouring is an illustrative radial estimate, not a 2D solution. The 175 plain rods are 139 РР manual, 12 ЛАР local-automatic and 24 АЗ emergency rods, which the sources do not tell apart. 24 of them read exactly 7 cm in both maps; they are probably fully withdrawn, but their scattered positions rule them out as the АЗ group, which would be laid out symmetrically like the АР and УСП rods.

INSAG-7 (p.142) notes that none of the three Soviet 3D models reproduced the overpower signal within 3 seconds, and that every model was extremely sensitive to the starting axial distribution. This one is too: lowering the water-column absorption by 7 % (0.30 → 0.28) stops the 01:22:30 state from exploding, and raising it by 7 % makes the 00:39 rods trip. Matching the record here comes from tuning a far simpler model to the documents. It is not proof that the model is right.

Связь с реактором потеряна · signals lost

On 26 April 1986 reactor No. 4 was destroyed at about 01:23:45.

The human cost

  • Killed that night: 2. Valery Khodemchuk, main circulation pump operator, died in the explosion; his body was never recovered. Vladimir Shashenok, a commissioning engineer, died of his injuries in Pripyat hospital that morning.
  • Acute radiation syndrome: 134 plant staff and emergency workers were diagnosed. 28 of them died in 1986, bringing the deaths that year to 30 (UNSCEAR). Further deaths among the survivors followed over the next decades.
  • Thyroid cancer: almost 20,000 cases were registered in 1991–2015 among people who were children or adolescents in 1986 in Belarus, Ukraine and the most affected parts of Russia. About a quarter (roughly 5,000) are probably due to radioactive iodine from the accident (UNSCEAR 2018). Most were treated successfully; at least nine children had died of it by 2005 (WHO).
  • Eventual cancer deaths are estimates, and they differ widely. The UN Chernobyl Forum (WHO, IAEA, 2005) projected up to about 4,000 among the 600,000 most exposed. The International Agency for Research on Cancer projected about 16,000 across Europe by 2065, with an uncertainty range of 6,700–38,000 (Cardis et al., 2006). Most scientific bodies place the toll in this range. Higher figures, such as 27,000 (Union of Concerned Scientists, 2011), 93,000 (Greenpeace, 2006) and 985,000 (Yablokov et al., Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences), rest on contested methods; the last is widely rejected.
